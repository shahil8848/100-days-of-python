Caesar Cipher Project
Welcome to the Caesar Cipher project! 🎉

What is a Caesar Cipher?
The Caesar Cipher is one of the oldest encryption techniques, named after Julius Caesar, who used it to keep his messages secret. The basic idea is simple: shift each letter of the alphabet by a certain number of positions. This project allows you to encode and decode messages using this fun and simple cipher.

Features
Encode your messages by shifting letters forward in the alphabet.

Decode your messages by reversing the shift.

Handles non-alphabet characters (spaces, punctuation, etc.) without altering them.

Allows you to choose your shift amount (how many letters to move).

Repeats the process as many times as you like. Keep encoding until your brain explodes 💥.

How It Works
Encoding:

The message you provide will be transformed by shifting each letter forward by the shift amount you specify.

Decoding:

If you want to decrypt a message, just provide the shift amount and the function will reverse the shift to get back to the original message.

Example:

Encoding "hello" with a shift of 3 becomes "khoor".

Decoding "khoor" with the same shift of 3 brings you back to "hello".
